# 看門狗：殘響 WATCH_DOGS: ECHO

**非官方同人作品，《看門狗：自由軍團》外傳，分成三部：前傳《殘響》是文字冒險，第一季《宵禁》和第二季《零時差》接著它往下走。英文、繁中、簡中三語。**
An unofficial fan work set after *Watch Dogs: Legion*, in three parts: the prequel *ECHO* is a text adventure, and Season 1 *Curfew* and Season 2 *Zero Day* carry on from it. Playable in English, Traditional Chinese and Simplified Chinese.

## 故事 Story

你是 ECHO，一個能「從資料重建任何人過去」的 AI，剛從實驗室逃出來，困在倫敦的 ctOS 網路裡。把你救出來的 DedSec 駭客失蹤了，而你即將被賣給一家軍事承包商，成為「目標引擎」。你必須潛入三個陌生人的記憶，找到她，並決定這項技術，以及你自己的去向。

You are ECHO, an AI built to reconstruct anyone's past from their data. The DedSec hacker who broke you out has vanished, and you were about to be sold to a military contractor as a targeting engine. Dive into three strangers' memories, find her, and decide what becomes of the technology — and of yourself.

## 怎麼玩 How to play

**前傳《殘響》**：用瀏覽器打開 `index.html` 就能玩，不需要安裝。**Prequel "ECHO"**: open `index.html` in any modern browser. No install, no build step.

- 可以打字輸入指令，也可以點建議按鈕。Type commands or tap the suggestions.
- 三種語言的指令都能用，例如 `hack laptop`、`駭入筆電`、`入侵旧笔记本`。Commands work in all three languages.
- 右上角可以隨時切換語言。Switch language any time, top right.
- 進度自動儲存在瀏覽器裡。Progress saves automatically in your browser.
- 遊玩時間約 40–60 分鐘，共 4 個結局。About 40–60 minutes, 4 endings.

**第一季《宵禁》**：打開 `s2.html`，或從前傳的標題畫面點進去。在武裝無人機宵禁下的倫敦重建 DedSec：五個行政區（康登開始、西敏結束，中間三區的順序自己決定），八個主線任務（先潛入、中場喘口氣，再用文字冒險打主要目標），招募街上的任何人；白天分成早上、中午、傍晚，可以一起混、練掉弱點、照顧傷員、幫街坊、踩點，隊友之間會變成朋友或死對頭，也會發生各種事（球賽、德比戰、Wrench、Darcy、艾登等客人來訪教技能，還有一些白天的奇遇）；晚上除了固定的行動，每天還有一兩晚限定的機會（攔補給車、洗威脅評分、搶藥、放假線索、監聽獅心、偷部署圖、護送一家人出去），好處各不相同；夜間行動可以快速結算，也可以在俯視的戰術地圖上親自指揮（駭客遠端開門、身體類翻牆制伏、社交類引開守衛、專長各有一手，特質和弱點都會影響走法），隊員會受傷、被捕，重傷沒人照顧也可能過世。結局有五種，依盟友、名單的處理方式和前傳的結局而不同。玩過前傳的話，會自動讀取前傳的結局；打到盟友帶來的結局，那一輪的隊伍可以帶進第二季。網址加上 `?seed=任意文字` 可以重現同一批路人。
**Season 1 "Curfew"**: open `s2.html`, or follow the link on the prequel's title screen. Rebuild DedSec in a London under armed-drone curfew: five districts (Camden first, Westminster last, the middle three in any order), eight main missions (get in, catch your breath at half time, then take the target in a text adventure), and anyone on the street can be recruited. Days have a morning, afternoon and evening for hanging out, training weaknesses away, looking after the wounded, helping the neighbours and scouting; teammates become friends or rivals, and things happen between them (match nights, derby-day fights, guests like Wrench, Darcy and Aiden teaching new skills, and a few strange encounters). Besides the regular operations, a couple of short-lived opportunities turn up each day (hit a supply van, wipe threat scores, free medicine, lay a false trail, tap Lionheart's radio, steal a deployment plan, get a family out), each paying off in its own way. Night operations can be quick-resolved or played on a top-down tactical map, where hackers open doors from range, physical types climb and take guards down, social types talk guards away, specialists use their trade, and traits and flaws change how everyone moves. Your people get hurt, get arrested, and without care a serious wound can be fatal. Five endings, shaped by your ally, what you do with the List, and how the prequel ended. If you finished the prequel, its ending carries over; reach one of the ally endings and that crew can follow you into Season 2. Add `?seed=anything` to replay the same people.

**第二季《零時差》**：打開 `s3.html`，或從前傳、第一季的標題畫面點進去。這一季是俯視的即時小隊動作 Roguelike，要用鍵盤和滑鼠玩（沒有手機版）。故事接在第一季白廳那一夜之後三個星期：獅心防務要把金絲雀碼頭地下的資料堡壘「基石」整座賣給 Broca Tech，裡面有四萬一千份重建檔、一個照 Echo 的樣板做的預測模型，還有宵禁期間照名單帶走的人。基石每天晚上重新預測闖入者，所以每一晚的房間和守衛都不一樣。你在安全屋的名冊裡挑三個隨機生成的倫敦人組成小隊（職業決定武器、裝備和專長，特質和弱點一樣有效），場上一次一個人，按 1、2、3 換人；倒下的人不會死，會被關進基石，之後要找到拘留室才救得回來，救出來的陌生人也會加入名冊。打完第一季的話，那一輪的隊伍會跟過來：還在的人直接進名冊，被抓走的人關在基石裡等你去救。槍只是其中一半，另一半是駭入：過載配電箱、放蒸汽、引爆油桶、發動無人車、升起路樁、劫持無人機和砲塔、讓守衛的耳機尖嘯，再衝上去制伏。陷阱打到還沒發現你的敵人會直接放倒，所以先佈置再動手；沒響警報就清場有額外獎勵。每間打完挑一個漏洞（41 種，全隊共用），門上寫著下一間的獎勵和狀況，路上有黑市、維修間和事件。三個區段各有一個首領，打倒核心之後有四個結局；有幾句台詞會跟著你在前傳和第一季的結局改變。每一晚都會留下殘響，可以換永久強化和招募志願者。網址加上 `?seed=任意文字` 可以重現同一串房間，加上 `&bot=1` 可以看機器人自己打（不會動存檔）。
**Season 2 "Zero Day"**: open `s3.html`, or follow the link on the prequel or Season 1 title screen. This season is a real-time, top-down squad action roguelike for keyboard and mouse (there is no phone version). The story picks up three weeks after the night in Whitehall that ends Season 1: Lionheart are selling Keystone, their data fortress under Canary Wharf, to Broca Tech. It holds 41,207 reconstructions, a prediction model built from Echo's template, and the people taken from the List during the curfew. Keystone predicts its intruders again every night, so the rooms and guards are different each time. In the safe house you pick three randomly generated Londoners from your roster (the job sets the weapon, gadget and skill, and traits and flaws still count). One is on the floor at a time, and 1, 2 and 3 swap between them. Anyone who goes down is not killed but locked up in Keystone, and comes back only when a later squad finds the holding cells; strangers you free there join the roster too. If you finished Season 1, that crew comes along: everyone still standing joins the roster, and anyone who was taken is held in Keystone until you get them out. The gun is only half of it. The other half is hacking: overload junction boxes, vent steam, blow up fuel drums, launch driverless cars, raise bollards, hijack drones and turrets, make a guard's earpiece shriek, then run in for the takedown. Traps put down any enemy who has not spotted you, so set them up before you strike; clearing a room with no alarm pays extra. After each room you pick an exploit (41 of them, shared by the squad), and each door shows the next room's reward and conditions. Along the way there are black markets, maintenance rooms and encounters. Each of the three sectors ends in a boss, and beating the core leads to one of four endings; a few lines change with the endings you reached in the prequel and Season 1. Every night leaves echoes to spend on permanent upgrades and on recruiting volunteers. Add `?seed=anything` to replay the same rooms, and `&bot=1` to watch the bot play (it leaves your save alone).

## 特色 Features

- **同步率 Sync**：在記憶中，要照當事人的個性行動。違背他，記憶就會崩塌。每段記憶的規則都不同。Act as the person whose memory you are in; break character and the memory collapses.
- **出血效應 Bleed**：讀越多人類記憶，ECHO 越像人。它會從「本系統」改口成「我」。The more memories ECHO reads, the more human it becomes.
- **群像劇與角色切換 Ensemble**：招募的同伴可以做 ECHO 做不到的事。Recruit allies and switch to them to act where an AI can't.
- **話題式對話 Topic-based dialogue**：`ask Ho-man about the money`。前面章節的線索會解鎖新話題。Clues from earlier chapters unlock new topics.
- **全程式繪製的畫面 Fully procedural visuals**：13 張場景插畫、Profiler 人物卡、全螢幕演出、合成音效，不使用任何外部素材。13 illustrated scenes, cinematic moments and synthesized sound — no external assets. 第二季《零時差》另外用了請圖像模型畫的原創插圖、頭像和俯視圖（`assets/s3/`），沒有使用任何遊戲的素材。Season 2 (Zero Day) also uses original illustrations, portraits and top-down sprites made with an image model (`assets/s3/`); no assets from any game are used.
- **客串 Cameos**：原作的熟面孔（不劇透）。A few familiar faces.

## 錄影與測試 Recording

網址後面加 `?jump=` 可以直接跳到某一章，例如 `index.html?jump=finale`。Append `?jump=` to start from a chapter, e.g. `index.html?jump=finale`.
可用 Available: `memory1` `chinatown` `memory2` `chapter3` `memory3` `finale` `choice`

自動測試只需要 Node.js，不用安裝任何套件。它會檢查三語文本是否齊全、把前傳的四個結局各打一遍，並測試第一季《宵禁》的引擎和第二季《零時差》的戰鬥、流程與平衡。每次推送到 GitHub 也會自動執行。
The automated check needs only Node.js and no packages. It checks that all three languages are complete, plays through all four prequel endings, and tests the Season 1 (Curfew) engine and the Season 2 (Zero Day) combat, run flow and balance. It also runs on every push to GitHub.

```bash
node tools/check.js
```

## 專案結構 Project structure

```text
index.html             頁面骨架與載入順序 page shell and load order
css/terminal.css       版面與演出 layout and effects
js/engine/             引擎，不含任何台詞 engine (no story text)
js/ui/                 文字輸出、插畫、演出與音效 output, art, effects, sound
js/main.js             標題畫面、輸入、存檔 title screen, input, saving
content/commands.js    三語指令別名 command aliases
content/scenes/        每個章節一個檔案 one file per chapter
content/locales/       三語文本 story text in three languages
content/dev-routes.js  章節跳轉路線 chapter-jump routes
content-s2/            第一季《宵禁》的資料 Season 1 "Curfew" data
s3.html, css/s3.css   第二季《零時差》的頁面與樣式 Season 2 "Zero Day" page and styles
content-s3/            《零時差》的資料表、劇本與三語文字 Zero Day data tables, script and text
assets/s3/             《零時差》的插圖、頭像與俯視圖（圖像模型生成）Zero Day illustrations, portraits and sprites (made with an image model)
js/s3/                 《零時差》：戰鬥模擬、房間生成、流程、畫面、音效 Zero Day: combat sim, room generator, run flow, rendering, sound
tools/check.js         自動測試 automated check
tools/check-s3.js      《零時差》的自動測試（check.js 會一起跑）Zero Day tests, run by check.js
tools/art-slice.py     《零時差》素材的去背與切圖（需要 Pillow、numpy）Zero Day art keying and slicing (needs Pillow and numpy)
```

檔名和程式裡的 s2、s3 是製作時的編號：s2 是第一季《宵禁》，s3 是第二季《零時差》。File names and code keep their working numbers: s2 is Season 1 (Curfew) and s3 is Season 2 (Zero Day).

想參與翻譯、名字表或路人秘密，請看 [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)。Want to help with translations, name tables or passer-by secrets? See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

內容檔用 `.js` 而不是 `.json`，因為瀏覽器不允許從本機檔案讀取 JSON，這樣才能直接雙擊 `index.html` 遊玩。
Content files are `.js` rather than `.json` so the game runs straight from the file system.

**新增內容 Adding content**：在 `content/scenes/` 新增場景（台詞只寫 key），在三份語言檔加上同一組 key，再到 `index.html` 加一行 `<script>`。Add a scene file with text keys only, add the keys to all three locales, and include the script in `index.html`.
